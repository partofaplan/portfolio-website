Editorial

How I Work

I.

Iterate, don't rebuild.

Small, steady improvements beat heroic rewrites. Pick the problems worth solving.

II.

Learn by breaking things.

Stand it up, push it until it fails, and write down what happened.

III.

Developer experience first.

A platform nobody adopts is a hobby. An engineer waiting on an environment is a platform bug.

IV.

Make systems tell the truth.

Build feedback loops into every stage, not after the first incident.
